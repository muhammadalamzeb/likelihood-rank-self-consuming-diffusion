Self-consuming training loops can induce model collapse. We study unlabeled likelihood ranking of synthetics in self-consuming DDPM training on imbalanced mixtures (workshop-scale; not ImageNet diffusion). Primary: top-k does not improve sliced W2 vs random-k at generation g=1 (Delta W2>0 on 38/38 cells; bootstrap 95% CI excludes zero). Secondary: after falsifying an earlier false-stability hypothesis, minority retention shows bottom>rand>top mainly at moderate imbalance rho=5 (exploratory n=10, 10/10, exact p=0.002; held-out seeds 10-14: 4/5, CIs exclude zero). Full order weaker at rho=7 (6/10) and rho=10 (4/13). Code: https://github.com/muhammadalamzeb/likelihood-rank-self-consuming-diffusion.

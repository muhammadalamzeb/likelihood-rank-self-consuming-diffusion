\begin{abstract}
Self-consuming training loops can induce \emph{model collapse}.
We study unlabeled likelihood ranking of synthetics in self-consuming DDPM training on imbalanced mixtures (workshop-scale; not ImageNet diffusion).
\textbf{Primary:} top-$k$ does \emph{not} improve sliced $W_2$ vs.\ random-$k$ at generation~$g{=}1$
($\Delta W_2>0$ on $38/38$ cells; bootstrap 95\% CI excludes zero).
\textbf{Secondary:} after falsifying an earlier ``false stability'' hypothesis, minority retention $r$ shows bottom$>$rand$>$top mainly at moderate imbalance $\rho{=}5$
(exploratory $n{=}10$, $10/10$, exact $p{=}0.002$; held-out seeds $10$--$14$: $4/5$, CIs still exclude zero for both contrasts).
Full order is weaker at $\rho{=}7$ ($6/10$) and $\rho{=}10$ ($4/13$).
Code: \url{https://github.com/muhammadalamzeb/likelihood-rank-self-consuming-diffusion}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
As synthetic data proliferates, generative models are increasingly trained on mixtures of real and model-generated samples.
Iterated self-consumption can cause \emph{model collapse}~\citep{shumailov2023,alemohammad2023,gerstgrasser2024}.
Mitigations include accumulating real data~\citep{gerstgrasser2024} and filtering synthetics~\citep{feng2024,cai2025}.
When the only score is the generator's own likelihood, how should one select synthetics?
We isolate an interventional selection policy at matched synthetic count and matched real-mix $\alpha$.

Caveats that apply throughout: this is a workshop empirical note on controllable mixtures; the retention ordering is post-hoc relative to an earlier falsified hypothesis (Section~\ref{sec:limit}); we do not claim ImageNet-scale generality.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1)~Primary powered $W_2$ negative result for top-$k$ at $g{=}1$ ($n{=}38$ cells).
(2)~Stratified retention at $\rho\in\{5,7,10\}$, with a labeled held-out seed block at $\rho{=}5$.
(3)~A lightweight account of why the full order weakens as $\rho$ grows (minority-mass floor).
(4)~Explicit contrast to siloed biased-verifier selection~\citep{qiao2026}.

\section{Related work}
\citet{shumailov2023,alemohammad2023} document collapse and quality-biased sampling (\emph{MAD}).
\citet{gerstgrasser2024} show accumulating real+synthetic data can avoid collapse.

\subsection{Self-consuming loop}
Train DDPM $f^{(g)}$ on $\mathcal{D}^{(g)}$, sample synthetics, mix fixed real buffer (fraction $\alpha$) with selected synthetics.

\subsection{Likelihood proxy and policies}
Score $s(x)$: mean denoising MSE over a $t$-grid (lower $=$ higher likelihood).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{top-$k$ / bottom-$k$ / random-$k$:} pool of size $k=\max(n_{\mathrm{syn}},\lfloor k_{\mathrm{frac}}N\rfloor)$ by lowest / highest / uniform scores; then draw $n_{\mathrm{syn}}$ and mix with $\alpha$ real.
\item \textbf{mix:} same $\alpha$, but synthetic portion is a uniform subsample of all $N$ synthetics (no rank filter).
\item \textbf{replace:} pure synthetic.
\end{itemize}
Thus mix $\neq$ random-$k$.

\subsection{Metrics}
\label{sec:metrics}
Nearest-mean mode masses; minority-mean $m$; retention $r=m^{(1)}/m^{(0)}$ (allowed $>1$).
Sliced $W_2$: mean squared 1D Wasserstein over \textbf{24} projections; $\le$400 synth and $\le$400 held-out real points.
Hypothesis tests: bootstrap 95\% CIs of the mean paired $\Delta$; Wilcoxon signed-rank with exact sign enumeration for $n{\le}16$ and a normal approximation for larger $n$.
Cells with sliced $W_2{>}10$ (pathological late-generation collapse) are excluded from $W_2$ tables only.

\paragraph{Bottom-$k$ quality cost.}
Mean sliced $W_2$ at $g{=}1$ ($\rho{=}5$, $n{=}15$): bottom $0.51$ $<$ rand $0.64$ $<$ top $0.80$.
Bottom-$k$ improves aggregate $W_2$ while retaining minorities---it is not a ``junk dump'' on this metric (same ordering at $\rho{=}7,10$).

\paragraph{Unranked mix baseline.}
Matched-$\alpha$ mix (no rank filter; Gerstgrasser-style fixed real fraction, not growing accumulation) at $\rho{=}5$, seeds $0$--$4$: mean $r{=}0.37$, $W_2{=}0.69$, between top and rand and worse than bottom on both metrics.

\paragraph{Proxy vs.\ exact GMM density.}
On $g{=}0$ synthetics ($\rho{=}5$, $n{=}5$), Spearman of $s(x)$ vs.\ $-\log p_{\mathrm{GMM}}(x)$ averages $0.44$ (range $0.33$--$0.51$);
top-$k$ Jaccard vs.\ exact density ranking averages $0.64$ at $k{=}N/2$.
The proxy is directionally informative but imperfect.

\section{Analysis: why the order weakens with $\rho$}
\label{sec:mech}
Top-$k$ keeps low-$s(x)$ synthetics, which under an imbalanced teacher are disproportionately majority-mode.
As $\rho$ grows, minority mass at $g{=}0$ already shrinks, so the ranked pool contains few minority candidates to prefer or discard: top/random/bottom selection become more similar, $r$ contrasts shrink, and the full bottom$>$rand$>$top pattern becomes seed-noisy---a minority-mass floor, not a contradiction of the $\rho{=}5$ regime.
Aggregate $W_2$ remains a poor minority monitor: top-$k$ is worse on $W_2$ at $g{=}1$ across the whole matrix even where the three-way $r$-order fails.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limit}
\textbf{Scale.} 2D/8D GMMs only for primary claims; CIFAR tiny-UNet is appendix-only; no ImageNet / CIFAR-10-LT / DiT result.
\textbf{Process.} Documented exploratory search ($>$100 killed directions in \texttt{docs/KILL\_LOG.md}) precedes this topic; within-topic, the original false-stability hypothesis was falsified and the retention claim revised (HARKing risk).
Mitigation: $W_2$ primary; stratified $\rho$; held-out seeds $10$--$14$ at $\rho{=}5$; bootstrap CIs.
\textbf{Other.} $\alpha$/$k_{\mathrm{frac}}$ ablations are $n{=}2$; mix baseline is $n{=}5$ at $\rho{=}5$ only; no growing-accumulation or external-verifier (Feng/Cai) baseline; no FID/precision--recall; no continuous $\rho$ sweep; Holm within $W_2$ generations and within paired $\rho{=}5$ contrasts.

\section{Conclusion}
Top-$k$ likelihood selection does not improve sliced $W_2$ at $g{=}1$ in this matrix.
Minority-retention ordering is strongest at moderate imbalance and weakens as minority mass floors---a mechanism-level boundary, not a universal law.

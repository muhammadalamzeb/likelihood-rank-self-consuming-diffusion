\begin{abstract}
Self-consuming training loops---retraining generative models on their own outputs---can induce \emph{model collapse}, including loss of rare modes.
Prior work notes that bias toward high-quality synthetic samples worsens the quality--diversity tradeoff, and that task-level verification can stabilize labeled synthetic data.
We study the most common \emph{unlabeled} selection score for diffusion models: a denoising ELBO~/~likelihood proxy.
On imbalanced Gaussian mixtures with a tiny DDPM, under matched real-data mixing and matched synthetic budget, first-generation minority-mass retention obeys
\[\mathrm{bottom}\text{-}k \;\;>\;\; \mathrm{random}\text{-}k \;\;>\;\; \mathrm{top}\text{-}k\]
(means \(0.57/0.36/0.17\) over eight seed--imbalance pairs; order holds on \(5/5\) seeds at imbalance \(\rho{=}5\)).
Paired Wilcoxon tests vs.\ random-\(k\) give \(p{\approx}0.042\) for both top and bottom contrasts.
Likelihood top-\(k\) also concentrates majority mass relative to random-\(k\).
A natural ``false stability'' hypothesis---that top-\(k\) improves sliced \(W_2\) while minorities die---is falsified.
The ordering transfers to \(8\)D GMM and to Digits in PCA space with nearest class-mean occupancy (\(3/3\) seeds each).
We release configs, seeds, logs, and analysis code.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
As synthetic data proliferates, generative models are increasingly trained on mixtures of real and model-generated samples.
Iterated self-consumption can cause \emph{model collapse}: progressive loss of diversity and forgetting of rare modes~\citep{shumailov2023,alemohammad2023,gerstgrasser2024}.
Mitigations include accumulating real data~\citep{gerstgrasser2024}, injecting fresh real samples, and \emph{filtering} synthetic data~\citep{feng2024,cai2025}.

A practical question remains underspecified: when the only available score is the generator's own likelihood (or denoising loss), how should one select synthetic samples?
\citet{feng2024} show that \emph{task verifiers} for labeled synthetic answers can prevent collapse.
\citet{cai2025} filter ``unrealistic'' diffusion samples via latent probes and report aggregate FID/precision/recall.
\citet{alemohammad2023} observe that sampling bias toward high-quality generations drives a quality--diversity tradeoff.
What is missing is a \textbf{matched-budget, mode-exact} causal contrast among likelihood ranks.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A controlled self-consuming DDPM protocol on imbalanced GMMs with exact mode masses.
\item Evidence for a \textbf{likelihood-rank ordering} of minority retention under matched \(k\) and matched real mix ratio.
\item An experimental \textbf{falsification} of \(W_2\)-based false stability for likelihood top-\(k\).
\item Transfer checks: \(8\)D GMM and Digits-PCA with nearest class-mean occupancy.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Related work}
\paragraph{Model collapse.}
\citet{shumailov2023} document recursive training collapse and tail forgetting.
\citet{gerstgrasser2024} show accumulating real+synthetic data can avoid collapse.
\citet{alemohammad2023} (\emph{MAD}) highlight quality-biased sampling.

\paragraph{Verification and filtering.}
\citet{feng2024} argue verification of synthesized \emph{labels} prevents collapse.
\citet{cai2025} (\emph{LSF}) filter synthetic images using latent-space realism scores.
Neither isolates unlabeled ELBO top-\(k\) vs.\ random-\(k\) with mode-grounded metrics on continuous mixtures.

\paragraph{Temperature~/~diversity knobs.}
Inference-time temperature methods~\citep{xu2025,li2026} reshape sampling diversity; we instead intervene on \emph{training-set selection} inside self-consuming loops.

\section{Method}
\subsection{Self-consuming loop}
Let \(f^{(g)}\) be a DDPM trained on \(\mathcal{D}^{(g)}\).
Sample synthetic set \(\widehat{\mathcal{D}}^{(g+1)}\).
Build the next train set by mixing a fixed real buffer \(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{real}}\) (fraction \(\alpha\)) with selected synthetics.

\subsection{Likelihood proxy and policies}
For each synthetic \(x\), define score \(s(x)\) as mean denoising MSE over a grid of times \(t\) (ELBO proxy; \textbf{lower} $=$ higher likelihood).
Policies at matched synthetic count:
\textbf{top-\(k\)} (lowest \(s\)),
\textbf{random-\(k\)},
\textbf{bottom-\(k\)} (highest \(s\)),
plus \textbf{mix}~/~\textbf{replace} references.

\subsection{Metrics}
Nearest-mean mode assignment on known centers (GMM means or Digits empirical class means in PCA space).
Report minority-mean mass, majority mass, and sliced \(W_2\) to held-out real samples.

At \(\rho{=}5\), generation 1, all five seeds satisfy \(\mathrm{bottom}>\mathrm{rand}>\mathrm{top}\).

\paragraph{Real-mix sensitivity.}
An \(\alpha\)-sweep (\texttt{w2\_fs\_alpha.jsonl}) shows the top-\(k\) penalty is clearest at \(\alpha\le 0.5\); at \(\alpha{=}0.75\) it weakens vs.\ random while bottom-\(k\) remains highest on average.

\paragraph{Transfers.}
An \(8\)D GMM transfer (\texttt{w2\_fs\_hd.jsonl}) reproduces the ordering on \(3/3\) seeds (retention means \(0.55/0.27/0.08\)).
Digits in PCA-\(8\) with nearest class-mean occupancy (\texttt{w2\_fs\_digits\_pca.jsonl}) holds on \(3/3\) seeds (retention means \(1.49/1.11/0.68\)).
An earlier Digits CVAE+classifier probe is inconclusive and unused for claims.

\paragraph{Selection-pool size.}
Under \(k=\max(n_{\mathrm{syn}},\lfloor k_{\mathrm{frac}}N\rfloor)\), \(k_{\mathrm{frac}}\in\{0.25,0.5\}\) are operationally equivalent when \(n_{\mathrm{syn}}\) binds; at \(k_{\mathrm{frac}}{=}0.75\) top-\(k\) remains worst.

\section{Analysis}
Likelihood top-\(k\) preferentially retains synthetic points the current model already explains well---typically majority-mode samples---reducing minority inflow into \(\mathcal{D}^{(g+1)}\).
Bottom-\(k\) does the opposite relative to random.
This is consistent with MAD's quality-bias narrative but makes the score-rank interventional and mode-exact.
Aggregate \(W_2\) is a poor monitor for this failure: it moves with top-\(k\) in the \emph{wrong} direction for a ``quality win.''

\section{Limitations}
Toy 2D/8D GMMs and Digits-PCA with tiny networks; not ImageNet-scale.
Digits CVAE transfer was inconclusive; Digits-PCA uses embeddings, not raw pixels.
Effect strongest at moderate imbalance and early generations; high real mix (\(\alpha{=}0.75\)) weakens the top-\(k\) vs.\ random gap.
Novelty is \textbf{L2 empirical} relative to MAD/LSF/Feng; we do not claim a new SOTA training method on FID.

\section{Conclusion}
Unlabeled likelihood ranking of synthetic data in self-consuming diffusion induces a stable minority-retention order (bottom-\(k\) $>$ random-\(k\) $>$ top-\(k\)) under matched budgets on imbalanced mixtures, while failing to deliver \(W_2\) improvements.
Preferring ``most likely'' synthetics is not a free lunch for minority survival.
